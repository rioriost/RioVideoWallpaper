## App Review notes

RioVideoWallpaper is a macOS menu bar utility for user-selected local video wallpapers and locally generated abstract looping wallpapers. No account, login, subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads, hosted user-generated content, cloud generation, or data collection is included.

Version 1.2.2 reorganizes General settings, adopts native settings tabs, improves smaller-window layouts, makes export and wallpaper actions clearer, and adds accessible control names and values. The preview follows the export aspect ratio. Existing video rendering, storage, permissions, and wallpaper playback remain unchanged.

Review steps:
1. Launch the app. On first launch, the generation editor opens.
2. Choose a Renderer, adjust the Seed and visual controls, and preview the result. Playback controls are below the preview.
3. Click Export. After export finishes, open Set as Wallpaper and choose Set to All Displays or Set on Display.
4. Use the menu bar Settings (Command-Comma) > General > Choose Video to select a local video. Use Video Generation to return to the editor.
5. Switch between General and Video Generation to verify that the current edits are retained.
6. General settings also contains Launch at Login and the generated-history folder controls.

Only user-selected files and folders are accessed through standard macOS file panels. Generated projects, thumbnails, and videos stay on the Mac. Launch at Login is optional. The Privacy Policy is available from the menu bar.
